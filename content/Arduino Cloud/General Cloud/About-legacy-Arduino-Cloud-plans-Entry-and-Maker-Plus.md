---
title: "About legacy Arduino Cloud plans (Entry and Maker Plus)"
---

Arduino Cloud plans underwent a restructuring to simplify the options available to makers and enterprises. As part of this update, the **Entry** and **Maker Plus** plans have been retired and are no longer available for new subscriptions.

In this article:

* [What happens to current subscribers?](#current-subscribers)
* [Legacy vs. current plans comparison](#comparison)
* [Upgrading from a legacy plan](#upgrades)

---

## What happens to current subscribers? {#current-subscribers}

If you are currently subscribed to a legacy **Entry** or **Maker Plus** plan, you can continue using your plan with all of its existing features, limits, and options. Your subscription will renew automatically as usual, provided your payment details remain valid.

However, if you choose to cancel your legacy plan or switch to a different plan, you will not be able to subscribe to these legacy plans again.

---

## Legacy vs. current plans comparison {#comparison}

Comparing legacy plans with our current offerings highlights how upgrading can increase your capacity and unlock new capabilities.

| Feature | Entry (Legacy) | Maker (Current Upgrade) | Maker Plus (Legacy) | Team (Current Upgrade) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Max Things / Devices** | 10 | **25** *(+150%)* | 100 | **100** |
| **Variables per Thing** | 10 | **Unlimited** (hard limit 100) | Unlimited (hard limit 100) | **Unlimited** (hard limit 100) |
| **Data Retention** | 15 days | **90 days (3 months)** | 365 days (1 year) | **365 days (1 year)** |
| **Users / Collaboration** | 1 user | **1 user** | 1 user | **Multi-user (Up to 50)** |
| **API Rate Limit** | 1 req/sec | **10 req/sec** | 10 req/sec | **Unlimited** (fair usage) |
| **Dashboard Sharing** | No | **No** | Yes | **Yes** |
| **Over-the-Air (OTA)** | Yes | **Yes** | Yes | **Yes** |
| **Web Editor Storage** | 1 GB | **1 GB** | 1 GB | **1 GB** |

## Upgrading from a legacy plan {#upgrades}

Upgrading to our current plan structure expands your limits and enables collaboration:

* **From Entry to Maker:** Upgrading to the current **Maker plan** increases your capacity to 25 Things, expands data retention to 90 days, and unlocks unlimited variables per Thing to support more complex projects.
* **From Maker Plus to Team:** Moving to the **Team plan** introduces [multi-user collaboration](https://docs.arduino.cc/arduino-cloud/business/arduino-cloud-for-business/#manage-your-spaces) (allowing up to 50 members to build and manage projects together) and removes strict API rate limits.

To upgrade or change your active plan, visit the [Arduino Cloud Plans page](https://cloud.arduino.cc/plans) or follow the step-by-step instructions in [Review, change, or cancel an Arduino Cloud plan](https://support.arduino.cc/hc/en-us/articles/4401881299090).
